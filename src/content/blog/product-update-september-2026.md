**What shipped in September 2026:** a fifth product for hotels, more European city pages, a cleaner phone menu, and privacy-first analytics. Here's what changed, and where each thing honestly stands.

## VoxStay for hotels (in development)

**VoxStay** is a new Vox product for hotels, and it's now on the site as an **in-development** product — real, with a working demo, but not something a hotel can switch on today. Its page says so plainly.

What it's designed to do: answer a hotel's phone in several languages, quote a real price including taxes, and text a secure payment link rather than ever reading a card number aloud on the call. If it isn't sure of an answer, it falls back to ringing the front desk.

### What it does not do yet

VoxStay has no live hotel behind it yet, so there are no results and no pricing to quote — every claim on its page is about how it's built, not about a hotel using it. It's in development, and we mark it that way rather than implying it's ready.

## French and Belgian city pages

We published city pages across France and Belgium, so an operator there can read about phone answering in their own language and market — the same honest, no-local-office framing as our UK pages. These are pilot markets; the product is live in Australia.

## A simpler menu, and privacy-first analytics

On phones and tablets, the whole site now shares **one menu bar and one way to switch region**, instead of a different layout per market. It's less to think about and works the same everywhere.

We also moved to **cookieless, privacy-first analytics** we host ourselves. We count visits to improve the site; we don't set marketing cookies or track you across the web. Details are in our [cookie policy](/legal/cookies/).

## New proof: Mazcina Resto-Bar

We published our first customer case study — [Mazcina Resto-Bar](/blog/mazcina-resto-bar-case-study/) in Darlinghurst, Sydney — with the owners' approval. It's a real venue using VoxTable and VoxOrder, in their own words.

## How to get it

Restaurants can [book a demo](/contact/) and hear Bella answer a live call. Hotels curious about VoxStay can register interest through the [VoxStay page](/products/voxstay/) — you'll get the pilot path, not a signup for something that isn't ready.
