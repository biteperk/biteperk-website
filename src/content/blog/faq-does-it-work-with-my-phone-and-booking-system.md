**You keep your number.** Setting up BitePerk doesn't mean porting numbers or swapping hardware — your existing line is forwarded to Bella, either on every call or only the ones your team can't get to. The bookings and orders she takes land in your BitePerk dashboard, ready for the floor.

## Your number, unchanged

Guests keep ringing the number on your door, your listings and your receipts. Forwarding is a setting on your existing phone service, so there's nothing physical to install and nothing for your regulars to relearn. You choose the mode: Bella on every call, or Bella only as backup when a call rings out.

## Where the bookings go

Every table Bella books and every order she takes appears in your BitePerk dashboard, so your team sees them alongside everything else and works one screen, not two. You decide who gets notified and how.

## A straight answer on other booking tools

We're careful here, because it's where a lot of vendors overpromise. BitePerk's own dashboard is where your Bella-taken bookings live today. If you already run a separate reservations tool, tell us during setup what you use and how your night runs — we'll talk you through exactly how the two sit together for your venue, rather than claiming a one-click integration that may not exist for your setup. An honest fit beats a broken "yes".

## Fits how you already work

You don't change your service to suit the software. Bella learns your hours, your booking rules and your menu, and works within them. If you'd rather she only picks up overflow at first, start there and widen it once you trust her.
