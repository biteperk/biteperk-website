## The challenge: the phone rings when nobody can reach it

Mazcina is a neighbourhood venue with a small, hands-on team. The kitchen is busy, the bar is making cocktails and plates are going out to tables. That is exactly when the phone starts ringing: people wanting a table tonight, a group asking about the weekend, a local calling in a pickup order on the way home.

In a room like this there is no spare person to stand by the phone. Whoever answers has to put down a tray, step away from the bar or leave a guest mid-conversation. More often the call simply rings out.

A missed call at a restaurant is rarely a caller who waits and tries again. It is a table that gets booked somewhere else down the street, or a takeaway order that goes to the next venue on the list. For a small venue those calls add up quickly, and nobody knows how many there were, because a call nobody answered leaves no record.

## The solution: VoxTable and VoxOrder, answered by Bella

Mazcina's owners set up two products from the BitePerk Vox family, both answered by the same voice: Bella, an AI phone host with a warm Australian voice.

- **[VoxTable](/products/voxtable/) handles bookings.** Bella answers, takes the name, party size, date and time, and puts the booking in the diary. Changes and questions are handled in the same call.
- **[VoxOrder](/products/voxorder/) handles takeaway and pickup.** Bella reads the menu the way the staff would, including substitutions, and sends the order through as a clean ticket with the caller's name and pickup details.
- **People still handle anything unusual.** When a request is outside what Bella should decide, such as a special event or a complicated dietary question, she takes the details and flags the team to call back. She doesn't guess.

Nothing changed for guests. They ring the number they already know. Calls are [forwarded to Bella](/blog/how-to-forward-your-restaurant-phone-to-an-ai-host/), so there was no new number to print and no app to download.

## How it runs day to day

For the team on the floor, the change is mostly what they no longer have to do. The phone doesn't pull anyone away from a table. Bookings don't have to be written on a notepad at the bar and typed up later. Takeaway orders aren't taken over the noise of service and then puzzled over in the kitchen.

Instead, the work arrives already done:

1. **A guest calls.** Bella answers on the first ring, whether the room is full or quiet.
2. **The request is captured properly.** A booking goes into the diary, or a takeaway order becomes a clean ticket.
3. **It lands on screen.** The team sees the booking or the order with the details they need, and gets on with service.
4. **Anything tricky becomes a callback.** Those calls are flagged with the caller's details, so the team decides how to handle them when they have a moment.

## The result: the calls they used to miss now get answered

The clearest way to describe the change is in the owners' own words:

> "Bella picks up the calls we used to miss. Bookings and takeaway orders just land on our screen."
>
> — **Camilo & Mauro**, owners, Mazcina Resto-Bar, Darlinghurst

What that means in practice:

- **No more calls ringing out during service.** The busiest part of the night is no longer when the venue is hardest to reach.
- **Bookings and orders arrive already captured.** Details are captured on the call, so there is less to re-type and less chance of a misheard order.
- **The team stays with the guests in the room.** Staff time goes to hospitality, not to the handset behind the bar.
- **Takeaway is covered by the same line.** Mazcina didn't need one tool for bookings and another for phone orders; one host handles both.

## Why this works for venues like Mazcina

Mazcina's setup is a good fit for many Sydney restaurants and bars. It works for venues that share three things:

- **A small team with no one spare for the phone.** The fewer hands there are, the more one missed call costs. There's more on this in [what missed calls cost your restaurant](/blog/what-missed-calls-cost-your-restaurant/).
- **Both bookings and takeaway coming through one number.** VoxTable and VoxOrder share the same host, so one line covers both kinds of call.
- **Peak calls that line up with peak service.** Guests ring when they're planning dinner, which is when you're serving it. An AI host that always answers fits that timing.
