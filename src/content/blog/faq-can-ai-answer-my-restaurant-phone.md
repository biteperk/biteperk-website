**Yes.** BitePerk's AI phone host, Bella, picks up your restaurant's calls in a natural Australian voice, answers the everyday questions and takes the booking or the order — then puts it straight on your team's screen. Anything she isn't sure of becomes a callback, not a guess.

## What "answering the phone" really means here

Most restaurant calls are the same handful of things: is there a table on Friday, what time do you close, can I order takeaway, do you do birthdays. Bella handles those start to finish. She isn't a menu of "press 1 for bookings" — she talks, listens and confirms, the way a good front-of-house person does when they have a second to breathe.

She works off what your venue actually does: your hours, your booking rules, your menu. So when a caller asks something specific — "do you have a table for eight at 7" — she checks and answers, rather than reading a script.

## Where a person still matters

Bella is deliberately built not to bluff. If a caller asks something outside what she knows, or wants something only a manager should decide, she takes their details and flags your team to ring back. That's the honest design: a warm handover beats a confident wrong answer that costs you a booking.

## You keep your number and your voice

Nothing about your phone number changes — your existing line is forwarded to Bella, so guests ring the number they already know. And the voice is Australian on purpose: a caller booking a table in Surry Hills shouldn't feel like they've reached an overseas call centre.

## Does it suit a small venue?

It suits small venues most of all. A big room has someone spare to grab the phone; a ten-table bistro doesn't, and the calls still come mid-service when every pair of hands is on the floor. That's exactly the gap Bella fills — she's on every call at once, and she never puts anyone on hold to seat a table.
