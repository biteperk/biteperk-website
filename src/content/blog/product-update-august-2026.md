**What shipped in August 2026:** a UK company so UK customers contract with a UK entity, and a new home for the VoxTable booking app. Both are smaller than they sound, and neither changes how your phone is answered.

## A UK company for UK customers

We registered **Biteperk Ltd** in England and Wales (company number 17379647). For a restaurant in the UK, it means the company you'd contract with, and that handles your data, is a UK entity — the details are on our UK site's company page.

### Why

If you run a venue in the UK, you should be dealing with a UK company, not a form on an overseas website. Registering the entity is the honest first step before offering pilots there.

### What it does not do yet

This is a **registered office, not a premises**. There is still no UK staff, no UK phone line and no local team — a registered office is an address on a filing, nothing more. The UK is a pilot market; the product is live in Australia. We don't claim a London office because we don't have one.

## The booking app moved

The VoxTable booking app — where your team sees and manages the bookings Bella takes — now lives at a new web address, `voxtable.biteperk.com.au`. If you use the app, you may notice the address in your browser; your login and your data are unchanged.

### Why

The app carries the product's name now, which is clearer than the old address. Old links keep working while the move settles, so nothing breaks if you have it bookmarked.

## How to get it

Nothing to do if you're an existing customer — same app, same login, same number. UK operators who want to try a pilot can [get in touch](/contact/); the UK site has the local details. Australian restaurants can [book a demo](/contact/) and hear Bella on a real call.
