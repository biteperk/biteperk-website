Every restaurant eventually hits the same wall: the phone rings during service and nobody can get to it. There are really only four ways to deal with it, and each makes a different trade between cost, coverage, and how the caller feels. Here's the honest comparison.

## The four options

**1. Let it ring / voicemail.** The default for most venues. Costs nothing upfront, and that's the only thing going for it. Diners deciding where to eat *tonight* don't leave a message — they hang up and call the next place. Voicemail is where bookings go to die.

**2. A staff member answers.** The traditional answer, and the best one *when someone's free*. The problem is timing: calls peak at exactly the moments your floor and kitchen are slammed. You're either pulling someone off a table to answer, or the call rings out anyway. And it stops the moment you close.

**3. A human answering service.** An offshore or local call centre takes your calls. It covers the hours, but quality is the catch — agents juggle dozens of unrelated businesses, don't know your menu or your room, and the accent often tells diners instantly the call has left the building. It's also usually priced per-call or per-minute, so it gets pricey fast. (Worth a read: [why an Australian voice matters](/blog/why-an-australian-voice-matters/) on your restaurant's phone.)

**4. An AI host.** A voice agent answers every call on the first ring, 24/7, knows your availability, and writes bookings straight to your diary. Modern ones sound genuinely natural and can be trained on your venue. The trade is that it's software — it shines at bookings, hours, and FAQs, and hands off the rare oddball call.

## So which should you pick?

- **A quiet venue with a host who's genuinely free at the phone?** Staff answering is hard to beat — keep it.
- **You only care about office-hours and have someone dedicated?** A human service can work, if you accept the per-call cost and the offshore feel.
- **You're losing calls during service, after close, or on days off — and want every caller to get a warm, on-brand answer without growing the wage bill?** An AI host is now the strongest all-round option for most restaurants.
