**Your records are stored in Australia, in Sydney.** Recordings of calls delete themselves after 30 days, and nothing from your calls is ever used to train AI models — ours or anyone else's. Below is the honest, specific version, including the one part people often get told wrong.

## What's stored, and where

The booking and order records Bella creates — names, times, party sizes, order details — are stored in Australia, in Sydney. That's where your guest data lives and stays.

Call recordings, where they're kept, auto-delete after 30 days. They exist only long enough to check quality and sort out the occasional "what did the caller actually say", then they're gone on a rolling basis without anyone having to remember to clear them.

## The part vendors fudge

Here's the honest bit. The live *conversation* — the moment-to-moment voice processing that lets Bella understand a caller and reply — runs on a voice-AI platform hosted in the United States. So the split is simple and we state it plainly: your stored records are in Australia, and the live call runs in the US. Some vendors blur that line; we'd rather give you the exact version than a comforting one.

What we can say plainly: nothing from your calls is used to train AI models — not ours, not the platform's, not a third party's.

## Why 30-day, self-deleting recordings matter

The less data you hold, the less there is to ever go wrong with. Recordings that delete themselves after 30 days mean your venue isn't sitting on a growing archive of guests' voices. It's a deliberate limit, not an oversight.
