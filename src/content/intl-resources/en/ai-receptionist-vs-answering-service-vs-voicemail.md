Every restaurant eventually hits the same wall: the phone rings during service and nobody can get to it. There are really only four ways to deal with it, and each makes a different trade between coverage, quality and how the caller feels. Here is the honest comparison.

## The four options

**1. Let it ring, then voicemail.** The default for most venues, and the only thing going for it is that it costs nothing upfront. Diners deciding where to eat *tonight* do not leave a message — they hang up and call the next place. Voicemail is where bookings go to die.

**2. A staff member answers.** The traditional answer, and the best one *when someone is free*. The problem is timing: calls peak at exactly the moments the floor and the kitchen are busiest. You are either pulling someone off a table to answer, or the call rings out anyway. And it stops the moment you close.

**3. A human answering service.** A call centre takes your calls. It covers the hours, but quality is the catch — agents juggle dozens of unrelated businesses, do not know your menu or your room, and callers can usually tell the call has left the building. It is also typically priced per call or per minute.

**4. An AI host.** A voice agent answers every call on the first ring, at any hour, knows your availability and writes bookings straight to your diary. The good ones sound natural and can be set up for your venue. The trade is that it is software: it excels at bookings, hours and the ordinary questions, and hands the rare unusual call to a person on your own line.

## So which should you pick?

- **A quiet venue with a host who is genuinely free at the phone?** Staff answering is hard to beat — keep it.
- **You only care about office hours and have someone dedicated?** A human service can work, if you accept the per-call pricing and the call-centre feel.
- **You are losing calls during service, after close or on days off — and want every caller to get a warm, on-brand answer without growing the wage bill?** An AI host is now the strongest all-round option for most restaurants.
