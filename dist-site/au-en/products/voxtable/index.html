Common questions

Answered plainly.

How accurate is Bella with Australian accents and place names?
Very. The model is trained on Australian English and we hand-tune it for the suburbs and venues we serve. Edge cases get caught by our Sydney team — not an overseas help desk.
Does VoxTable plug into my booking system?
Today we sync to your VoxTable diary directly; integrations with the major Australian booking platforms are on the roadmap. Existing bookings stay yours and can be exported at any time.
What happens to call recordings and data?
Audio is processed in Australia, stored only as long as needed to confirm the booking, and never used to train models outside Biteperk. We are Privacy Act compliant.
Can I cancel if it doesn't work for my venue?
Yes — VoxTable is month to month, no lock-in. You can cancel any time and your data is exported within 24 hours.
How is the price so low?
We built VoxTable from the ground up for hospitality, on infrastructure we run ourselves. Flat $80 a month, unlimited calls. No per-cover fees, ever.
Where is support based?
Sydney. 9am to 9pm AEST, by phone or email, with a human who has answered restaurant phones before.
