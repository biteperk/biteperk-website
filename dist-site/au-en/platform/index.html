One dashboard, six jobs

The same names you'll see in the sidebar.

Nothing here is a mock-up feature. These are the six panels of the dashboard every Vox venue signs into — described exactly as you'll find them.

Live Feed

Every call, the moment it happens.

The feed shows each call as Bella takes it — the transcript, the recording, the caller's sentiment, and whether it was handled end-to-end without a transfer. And when she isn't sure and takes a message instead, the callback flag sits right there in the feed: you see the misses as clearly as the wins.

Booking Log

Every booking, with its source call attached.

New bookings, changes and cancellations land in one log — each one linked back to the call that created it. No reconciling scribbled notes at the pass; if a guest says "but I rang and changed it", the answer is one tap away.

Analytics

Your numbers, not a black box.

Booking trends, peak-hour patterns and call outcomes — with PDF reports for your accountant and CSV export of the raw data whenever you want it. Analytics grow with your plan, from the essentials on Solo to deeper call-quality and peak-hour insights on Pro and up.

Live Tables

The floor, live.

For venues running table ordering, Live Tables shows every table's state in real time — who's seated, what's ordered, what's ready — so the front of house and the pass are always looking at the same picture.

Kitchen

Orders straight to the pass.

A kitchen display screen shows orders the moment they're placed — the same clean ticket VoxOrder promises, on a screen built for a working kitchen rather than a printer spitting dockets.

Manage Menu

Your menu, from a photo.

Point a camera at your printed menu and the dashboard reads it in — items, prices, variants. Edit once and it's live everywhere Bella and the ordering screens use it. No retyping the specials every week.

Your whole team, each with their own view.

Owner, manager, staff and kitchen roles come built in — everyone gets their own login and sees exactly what their job needs. Running more than one venue? The Group plan puts up to three locations in one dashboard with roll-up reporting, and Enterprise goes beyond that.

And it's all held to the same standard as the calls themselves: Australian data residency, Privacy Act compliant, and your data exported to you within 24 hours if you ever leave.

Common questions

The dashboard, answered plainly.

Do I get the dashboard on the $80 plan?
Yes. Every VoxTable plan includes the dashboard — the live call feed, transcripts and recordings, the booking log, and unlimited dashboard users are part of Solo at $80/month. Deeper analytics and multi-venue roll-ups come with the higher tiers.
Can my staff have their own logins?
Yes — and they're role-based. Owners, managers, staff and kitchen each get their own login and see what their role needs: the kitchen sees orders, staff see the floor and bookings, managers and owners see everything including analytics and billing.
Can I export my data?
Yes. Call and booking data exports to CSV from the dashboard, analytics reports export to PDF, and if you ever cancel, your bookings and data are exported to you within 24 hours. Your data stays yours.
Where does the dashboard data live?
In Australia. Call audio is processed onshore, kept only as long as needed to confirm the booking, and never used to train models outside Biteperk. Biteperk is Privacy Act compliant.
Do I need to install anything?
No. The dashboard runs in any browser — phone, tablet or the laptop in the office — and the kitchen display runs on any spare tablet or screen. There's no hardware to buy and nothing to install.
